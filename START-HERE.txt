DID GERMANY'S NUCLEAR PHASE-OUT INCREASE CO2 EMISSIONS?
A classroom activity for four breakout groups.

YOU DO NOT NEED TO UNDERSTAND ANY OF THESE FILES.

To put this online for your students, follow the walkthrough document
("Getting it online, step by step"). The short version:

  1. Make a free GitHub account.
  2. Create a new repository and drag ALL of these files into it.
  3. Make a free Render account, connect it to that repository.
  4. Render gives you a web address. That is your student link.

There is no software to install and nothing to type into a terminal.

Two links come out of it:
  Students      https://your-address.onrender.com/
  Facilitator   https://your-address.onrender.com/facilitator?key=YOUR-KEY

Keep the facilitator link to yourself. Anyone with it can pause the
rooms and export answers.
